This tutorial compares three recent models that predict depth from a single photo: Depth Anything V2, DepthAnything-AC, and Depth Anything 3. I ran their Small versions on the same fifteen photos, then repeated the tests with dimming and blur. AC is adapted from V2, so I wanted to compare the two on darker and blurred photos. The guides explain the methods and show the results through a mirror, a traffic scene, and a kitchen. I ran the released checkpoints for this project.

This tutorial compares three recent monocular depth models and how they respond to dimming and blur.

Monocular depth estimation predicts depth from a single photograph. I compared Depth Anything V2 Small, DepthAnything-AC Small, and Depth Anything 3 Small on the same 15 photos, then repeated the comparison with dimming and blur.

Research question

When dimming and blur change a photo, which models keep the near/far ordering correct? How does the adapted AC checkpoint compare with V2?

The guides explain how foundation models are used for depth estimation and how researchers adapt them. I tested the released models using annotated point pairs and six indoor scenes with sensor depth.

The main examples are a mirror where the models disagree, a traffic scene changed by dimming, and a kitchen with sensor depth for comparison.
