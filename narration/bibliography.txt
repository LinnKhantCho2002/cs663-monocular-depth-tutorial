The bibliography links directly to primary papers and datasets, with numbers matching first appearance in the lesson sequence. Each entry identifies the work, its authors or institutional source, its date, its relevance, and a reliability assessment. A peer-reviewed venue does not make every claim universally applicable, and an arXiv posting alone is not peer review. Author-provided benchmarks should be distinguished from independent evaluation. The tutorial's original diagrams and local prediction renders are identified separately from borrowed photographs. Photo captions preserve dataset and filename attribution, and watermarks remain visible. Model licenses do not automatically cover the source photographs. The downloadable provenance pins revisions and checksums so a reader can identify exactly what was studied.
