The main difference I'm studying is how the models learn depth. V2 uses pretrained image features and depth labels generated by a teacher. AC adapts V2 to keep predictions consistent when appearance changes. DA3 uses a different approach to geometry; I tested its Small model with one image. In the traffic example, dimming changes V2's answer even though the objects haven't moved. AC and DA3 keep all three pairs correct on that photo. Consistency alone isn't enough, though: a model could keep the same wrong answer. The point labels let us check both stability and correctness.
