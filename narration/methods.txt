These three models use different training approaches. V2 starts with pretrained image features and learns depth from labels generated by another model, called a teacher. AC's authors adapt V2 to preserve predictions through appearance changes. DA3 is designed to work with one or several views. In this experiment, I gave it one image at a time. Comparing the original and changed predictions shows how stable the answers are. Comparing those answers with the dataset labels shows which ones are correct.
