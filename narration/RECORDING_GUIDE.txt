Page narration
==============
Use these scripts as a starting point and adjust them to your own speaking style.

1. Open a lesson and expand "Page narration: listen, read, or record".
2. Open "Record your own narration". Press Record and allow microphone access.
3. Read the script. Press Stop, then listen back.
4. Choose Record again for a retake, or Download clip to keep it.
5. Check your Downloads folder before moving to the next lesson.

Clips stay local. Download before leaving the page so you do not lose a take.
Each script takes roughly 40–60 seconds; allow extra time for retakes.

The eight files are named for their pages:
index (introduction), start (mirror lesson), geometry, methods, evaluation, experiments, conclusions, bibliography.
Your browser chooses .webm, .m4a or .ogg. Keep the original extension.

Adding the clips
Place one final clip per page in site/narration/.
From the project folder run:
    .venv/bin/python scripts/build_site.py --content-only
Supported formats: .m4a, .webm, .ogg, .mp3, .wav, .mp4.
Check playback locally, publish the update, and check the public players.

If the browser cannot record, use Voice Memos and export each page's clip.
Record in a quiet room at a consistent distance from the microphone.

Pronunciation
RGB: R G B; DPT: D P T; DINO: dee-no; NYU: N Y U;
DA3: Depth Anything Three; AC: A C; AbsRel: absolute relative error.

Before keeping a take, check that the explanation matches the lesson:
- The outputs are relative inverse depth, not meters.
- NYU scores use the reference to fit scale and shift.
- The AC checkpoint was adapted by its authors; this project did not train it.
- The explorer displays saved runs.

These clips provide page narration once they are published. The full spoken presentation video is a separate deliverable.
