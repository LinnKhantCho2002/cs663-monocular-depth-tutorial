The room diagram shows why physical scale is difficult to recover from one photo. Double the size of every object and its distance from the camera, and the image can stay the same. Depth models use cues such as overlap, perspective, and familiar objects to estimate which surfaces are closer. The models tested here produce relative values. In the maps, yellow marks the closer end of each prediction's color range. Each map uses its own minimum and maximum, so the colors need to be read within that map.
