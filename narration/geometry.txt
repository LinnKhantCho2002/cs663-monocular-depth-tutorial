If I make a room twice as big and move everything twice as far from the camera, could the photo look the same? Try the first question before reading the equation. This is why estimating near and far is easier than recovering meters from one image. In our inverse-depth maps, a larger value means closer, but the numbers have an arbitrary scale. The colors question is worth trying too. Each map uses its own range, so matching colors can be misleading. With glass or a mirror, we also have to ask which surface the prediction describes.
