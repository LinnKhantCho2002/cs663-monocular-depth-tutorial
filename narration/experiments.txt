This page lets you compare the same photo under dimming and blur. The original photo stays visible while you change the input condition. Each choice loads the model output saved for that input. In the traffic example, V2 gets all three pairs right on the original, then one right under severe dimming. AC keeps all three right. You can switch models to compare their predictions on the same photo. The optional point inspector reads other locations in the saved map. The kitchen controls switch between the prediction, sensor reference, and error image.
