This tutorial is about monocular depth estimation, which means predicting depth from one photograph. For this project, I reviewed recent papers on foundation models and how researchers adapt them for depth prediction. I also compared three released models: Depth Anything V2 Small, DepthAnything-AC Small, and Depth Anything 3 Small. We'll begin with a mirror photo, then look at the geometry, model methods, and evaluation. The traffic and kitchen examples show how we can check the predictions instead of relying on how a map looks. You can try the questions and explore the saved results without installing anything.
