This project is about estimating depth from one photograph. I compared Depth Anything V2 Small, DepthAnything-AC Small, and Depth Anything 3 Small on the same images. One example is a car mirror: V2 and AC get an annotated near and far pair wrong, while DA3 gets it right. That difference can be easy to miss in the full maps. The tutorial starts with camera geometry and the meaning of relative depth, then explains how pretrained models are adapted for depth estimation. After that, you can explore the comparison and the sensor-reference examples. The browser loads predictions saved from local runs. It gives you controls for inspecting them, while the accompanying Python implementation handles fresh inference. Allow about twenty to twenty-seven minutes for the tutorial and examples.
