This tutorial is about how recent depth models respond to dimming and blur. Monocular depth estimation means predicting depth from one photograph. I compared V2 Small, AC Small, and DA3 Small on fifteen photos, using the same original and changed inputs for each model. AC is adapted from V2, so I wanted to see whether it kept more near and far answers correct. The guides cover the model papers, the experiment, and the results. The main examples are a mirror, a traffic scene, and a kitchen. I used the published models; I didn't train them myself.
