This project asks how recent monocular depth models behave when an image becomes difficult. These models predict depth from one photograph. I compared V2 Small, AC Small, and DA3 Small on the same fifteen photos, then repeated the test with dimming and blur. My main question is which models keep the near and far ordering correct, and how the adapted AC checkpoint compares with V2. The paper review explains foundation models and adaptation. The mirror, traffic, and kitchen examples let us examine the evidence. Geometry provides the background. I used released checkpoints, and my contribution is the comparison and analysis.
