The bibliography links directly to primary papers and datasets, with numbers matching first appearance in the lesson sequence. Each entry identifies the work, its authors or institutional source, its date, its relevance, and a reliability assessment. A peer-reviewed venue does not make every claim universally applicable, and an arXiv posting alone is not peer review. Author-provided benchmarks should be distinguished from independent evaluation. The tutorial's original diagrams and local prediction renders are identified separately from borrowed photographs. References follow their first appearance along the recommended lesson order. Paper claims, author-provided benchmarks, and our local measurements are different evidence sources.

Paper metadata and project links were checked October 5, 2026. Publication status below reflects the available primary records. An arXiv page provides a direct paper link, but posting there alone is not peer review. Every reference includes why it is relevant and what to question when relying on it.

[1] Depth Map Prediction from a Single Image using a Multi-Scale Deep Network

David Eigen; Christian Puhrsch; Rob Fergus · 2014/06/09 · NeurIPS 2014

Paper record · Direct PDF

Synopsis: Early coarse-to-fine neural depth prediction; introduces the historical motivation for combining scene context and local refinement.

Reliability: Peer-reviewed foundational work. Its architecture and test domains do not establish the behavior of current checkpoints.

[2] Vision Transformers for Dense Prediction

René Ranftl; Alexey Bochkovskiy; Vladlen Koltun · 2021/03/24 · ICCV 2021

Paper record · Direct PDF

Synopsis: Dense prediction from transformer representations through spatial reassembly and multiscale fusion.

Reliability: Peer-reviewed method paper with official code. Architecture evidence should be separated from downstream checkpoint-specific results.

[3] Depth Anything V2

Lihe Yang; Bingyi Kang; Zilong Huang; Zhen Zhao; Xiaogang Xu; Jiashi Feng; Hengshuang Zhao · 2024/06/13 · NeurIPS 2024

Paper record · Direct PDF

Synopsis: Teacher/student learning combines precise synthetic depth supervision and real-image pseudo-labels.

Reliability: Peer-reviewed, with public code and data. Author benchmarks are valuable primary evidence; training overlap in our sample remains unaudited.

[4] Repurposing Diffusion-Based Image Generators for Monocular Depth Estimation

Bingxin Ke; Anton Obukhov; Shengyu Huang; Nando Metzger; Rodrigo Caye Daudt; Konrad Schindler · 2023/12/04 · CVPR 2024

Paper record · Direct PDF

Synopsis: Repurposes a latent diffusion image model for image-conditioned affine-invariant depth estimation.

Reliability: Peer-reviewed primary paper. Sampling settings and output conventions matter; no local comparison with Marigold was performed.

[5] Metric3Dv2: A Versatile Monocular Geometric Foundation Model for Zero-shot Metric Depth and Surface Normal Estimation

Mu Hu; Wei Yin; Chi Zhang; Zhipeng Cai; Xiaoxiao Long; Kaixuan Wang; Hao Chen; Gang Yu; Chunhua Shen; Shaojie Shen · 2024/03/22 · IEEE TPAMI 46(12), 10579–10596, 2024

Paper record · Direct PDF

Synopsis: Metric depth and surface normals across camera settings through canonical camera-space reasoning.

Reliability: Peer-reviewed journal paper and official implementation. Metric claims depend on camera conventions; we did not reproduce its benchmark.

[6] Depth Pro: Sharp Monocular Metric Depth in Less Than a Second

Aleksei Bochkovskii; Amaël Delaunoy; Hugo Germain; Marcel Santos; Yichao Zhou; Stephan R. Richter; Vladlen Koltun · 2024/10/02 · ICLR 2025

Paper record · Direct PDF

Synopsis: Multiscale transformer metric depth with sharp boundaries and focal-length estimation.

Reliability: Peer-reviewed paper with public code. Published hardware timing cannot be transferred to our laptop or a phone.

[7] Depth Anything at Any Condition

Boyuan Sun; Modi Jin; Bowen Yin; Qibin Hou · 2025/07/02 · arXiv preprint, July 2025

Paper record · Direct PDF

Synopsis: Unlabeled perturbation-consistency fine-tuning and spatial-distance constraints for appearance robustness.

Reliability: Primary research preprint; no reviewed venue was verified. Our checkpoint study supplies limited local evidence, not a reproduction of all author claims.

[8] Depth Anything 3: Recovering the Visual Space from Any Views

Haotong Lin; Sili Chen; Junhao Liew; Donny Y. Chen; Zhenyu Li; Guang Shi; Jiashi Feng; Bingyi Kang · 2025/11/13 · arXiv technical report, November 2025

Paper record · Direct PDF

Synopsis: Any-view geometry with depth/ray reasoning; a family of distinct checkpoint variants.

Reliability: Primary technical report and official code. No reviewed venue was verified; our one-view Small test excludes broader family capabilities.

[9] DepthART: Scaling Foundation Monocular Depth to Tiny Models

Feng Xue; Wu Chen; Mingshuai Zhao; Guofeng Zhong; Anlong Ming; Haozhe Wang; Dianqiao Lei; Zhaowen Lin; Haiyang Zhang; Nicu Sebe · 2026/07/19 · ACM Multimedia 2026; acceptance reported on arXiv

Paper record · Direct PDF

Synopsis: Tiny-model depth distillation and camera-oriented adaptation motivate the efficiency discussion.

Reliability: Recent primary paper; acceptance is reported by the authors' record. No checkpoint or device measurements were executed here.

[10] NYU Depth V2 / Indoor Segmentation and Support Inference from RGBD Images

Nathan Silberman, Pushmeet Kohli, Derek Hoiem, Rob Fergus · ECCV 2012; official dataset page

Primary source

Synopsis: Indoor RGB and Kinect depth with filled labels and raw projected sensor measurements. Source of six reference examples.

Reliability: Institutional primary source. Kinect holes and filled labels require an explicit scoring mask; our selected records are not a verified held-out split.

[11] KITTI Depth Prediction Benchmark

KITTI Vision Benchmark Suite · official benchmark; depth completion/prediction work, 2017–2018

Primary source

Synopsis: Driving imagery and LiDAR-derived depth references provide an outdoor evaluation setting.

Reliability: Official benchmark specification. Sparse support and driving scenes differ from NYU; no KITTI predictions were run in this project.

[12] Hypersim: A Photorealistic Synthetic Dataset for Holistic Indoor Scene Understanding

Mike Roberts, Jason Ramapuram, Anurag Ranjan, Atulit Kumar, Miguel Angel Bautista, Nathan Paczan, Russ Webb, Joshua M. Susskind · ICCV 2021

Primary source

Synopsis: Rendered indoor scenes and geometric annotations support controlled synthetic supervision and evaluation.

Reliability: Official research repository. Rendered range and camera-axis depth require compatible conventions; synthetic coverage does not remove domain shift.

[13] DA-2K: Depth Anything V2 Evaluation Dataset

Depth Anything V2 authors · 2024 · official dataset and annotation documentation

Primary source

Synopsis: Diverse photographs with annotated point-pair depth ordering. Source of the 15 selected images and 35 reference pairs.

Reliability: Primary author-provided dataset. Pair annotations are not dense metric ground truth, and photograph ownership is distinct from model licensing.
