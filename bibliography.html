All the papers and datasets used in the tutorial are linked below.

[1] DA-2K: Depth Anything V2 Evaluation Dataset

Depth Anything V2 authors · 2024 · official dataset and annotation documentation

Primary source

Dataset: Diverse photographs with annotated point-pair depth ordering. Source of the 15 selected images and 35 reference pairs.

Source note: The authors' dataset and annotations. Labels check point ordering; they do not give distances across the image.

[2] Depth Map Prediction from a Single Image using a Multi-Scale Deep Network

David Eigen; Christian Puhrsch; Rob Fergus · 2014/06/09 · NeurIPS 2014

Paper record · Direct PDF

Method: Estimates scene depth with a coarse network, then refines local detail.

Source note: Peer-reviewed. Used here for the early coarse-to-fine design.

[3] Vision Transformers for Dense Prediction

René Ranftl; Alexey Bochkovskiy; Vladlen Koltun · 2021/03/24 · ICCV 2021

Paper record · Direct PDF

Method: Reassembles transformer features into spatial maps and fuses multiple resolutions for dense prediction.

Source note: Peer-reviewed, with official code. Used here to explain the decoder.

[4] Depth Anything V2

Lihe Yang; Bingyi Kang; Zilong Huang; Zhen Zhao; Xiaogang Xu; Jiashi Feng; Hengshuang Zhao · 2024/06/13 · NeurIPS 2024

Paper record · Direct PDF

Method: Trains students on real-image depth pseudo-labels from a synthetically supervised teacher.

Source note: Peer-reviewed, with public code and data. Training overlap with my selected photos is unknown.

[5] Repurposing Diffusion-Based Image Generators for Monocular Depth Estimation

Bingxin Ke; Anton Obukhov; Shengyu Huang; Nando Metzger; Rodrigo Caye Daudt; Konrad Schindler · 2023/12/04 · CVPR 2024

Paper record · Direct PDF

Method: Adapts a latent diffusion model for image-conditioned, affine-invariant depth.

Source note: Peer-reviewed. I reviewed the method but did not run it.

[6] Metric3Dv2: A Versatile Monocular Geometric Foundation Model for Zero-shot Metric Depth and Surface Normal Estimation

Mu Hu; Wei Yin; Chi Zhang; Zhipeng Cai; Xiaoxiao Long; Kaixuan Wang; Hao Chen; Gang Yu; Chunhua Shen; Shaojie Shen · 2024/03/22 · IEEE TPAMI 46(12), 10579–10596, 2024

Paper record · Direct PDF

Method: Uses canonical camera-space transformations to estimate metric depth and surface normals across camera settings.

Source note: Peer-reviewed, with official code. Its camera conventions affect comparisons with sensor depth.

[7] Depth Pro: Sharp Monocular Metric Depth in Less Than a Second

Aleksei Bochkovskii; Amaël Delaunoy; Hugo Germain; Marcel Santos; Yichao Zhou; Stephan R. Richter; Vladlen Koltun · 2024/10/02 · ICLR 2025

Paper record · Direct PDF

Method: Predicts sharp metric depth using a multiscale transformer, with focal-length estimation.

Source note: Peer-reviewed, with public code. Published speed results use the authors' hardware and setup.

[8] Depth Anything at Any Condition

Boyuan Sun; Modi Jin; Bowen Yin; Qibin Hou · 2025/07/02 · arXiv preprint, July 2025

Paper record · Direct PDF

Method: Adapts V2 on unlabeled images using perturbation consistency and spatial-distance constraints.

Source note: Preprint; no peer-reviewed venue verified. I tested the released Small model on this sample.

[9] Depth Anything 3: Recovering the Visual Space from Any Views

Haotong Lin; Sili Chen; Junhao Liew; Donny Y. Chen; Zhenyu Li; Guang Shi; Jiashi Feng; Bingyi Kang · 2025/11/13 · arXiv technical report, November 2025

Paper record · Direct PDF

Method: Estimates any-view geometry through a joint depth/ray representation, with several checkpoint variants.

Source note: Technical report with official code; no peer-reviewed venue verified. I tested Small with one image.

[10] DepthART: Scaling Foundation Monocular Depth to Tiny Models

Feng Xue; Wu Chen; Mingshuai Zhao; Guofeng Zhong; Anlong Ming; Haozhe Wang; Dianqiao Lei; Zhaowen Lin; Haiyang Zhang; Nicu Sebe · 2026/07/19 · ACM Multimedia 2026; acceptance reported on arXiv

Paper record · Direct PDF

Method: Studies tiny-model depth distillation and camera-specific adaptation.

Source note: The arXiv record reports acceptance at ACM Multimedia 2026. I reviewed the paper but did not test the model.

[11] NYU Depth V2 / Indoor Segmentation and Support Inference from RGBD Images

Nathan Silberman, Pushmeet Kohli, Derek Hoiem, Rob Fergus · ECCV 2012; official dataset page

Primary source

Dataset: Indoor RGB and Kinect depth with filled labels and raw projected sensor measurements. Source of six reference examples.

Source note: Official dataset source. Scoring uses sensor-supported pixels; these six scenes are not a verified test split.

[12] KITTI Depth Prediction Benchmark

KITTI Vision Benchmark Suite · official benchmark; depth completion/prediction work, 2017–2018

Primary source

Dataset: Driving imagery and LiDAR-derived depth references provide an outdoor evaluation setting.

Source note: Official benchmark source. I reviewed it but did not evaluate models on KITTI.

[13] Hypersim: A Photorealistic Synthetic Dataset for Holistic Indoor Scene Understanding

Mike Roberts, Jason Ramapuram, Anurag Ranjan, Atulit Kumar, Miguel Angel Bautista, Nathan Paczan, Russ Webb, Joshua M. Susskind · ICCV 2021

Primary source

Dataset: Rendered indoor scenes and geometric annotations support controlled synthetic supervision and evaluation.

Source note: Official research repository. Its synthetic scenes and distance conventions differ from real sensor datasets.
